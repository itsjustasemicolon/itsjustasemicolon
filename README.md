## About Me

I'm a **final-year Information Technology undergraduate at Jadavpur University** who enjoys turning ideas and logic into practical software.

My interests sit at the intersection of **software development, problem solving and computer science fundamentals**. I enjoy working across the stack, understanding how systems behave under the hood, and continuously improving the way I design and write software.

- Currently strengthening **Data Structures & Algorithms** and **Core CS**
- Learning **Low-Level Design** and **High-Level Design**
- Building and improving **full-stack applications**
- Interested in **software engineering, system design and scalable applications**
- Occasionally convincing myself that one missing semicolon is the root cause of everything

> **A semicolon is the difference between a functional application and a mental breakdown.**
